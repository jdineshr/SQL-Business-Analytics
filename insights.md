# 📊 Business Findings – Swiggy Food Analysis

## 🎯 Objective
To analyze food delivery data and identify key trends in customer behavior, restaurant performance, and pricing patterns to improve revenue, customer experience, and operational efficiency.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Top Performing Restaurants
- Top 10 restaurants contribute ~30–35% of total orders
- Highly rated restaurants (4.0+) receive significantly more orders
- Popular cuisines such as North Indian and Chinese dominate sales

📌 Insight:
Customer ordering decisions are highly influenced by restaurant ratings and cuisine preferences, leading to concentration on a few top-performing restaurants.

---

### 2. Order Volume Trends (Price-Based)
- Highest order volume observed in the ₹100–₹199 price range
- Secondary demand in ₹200–₹299 range
- Low demand for high-priced dishes

📌 Insight:
Customer demand is highly price-sensitive, with a strong preference for affordable meal options. This indicates the need for efficient inventory and menu planning for low-price, high-demand items.

---

### 3. Customer Ordering Behavior
- Majority of users place 1–2 orders per week
- Repeat customers contribute ~65% of total revenue
- High-value customers order more frequently on weekends

📌 Insight:
Customer retention is strong; however, increasing order frequency among existing users presents a key growth opportunity.

---

### 4. Revenue & Pricing Analysis
- Higher-priced restaurants generate higher revenue per order but lower order volume
- Combo meals and promotional offers increase average order value

📌 Insight:
There is a clear trade-off between order volume and profitability, requiring a balanced pricing and discount strategy.

---

### 5. Location-Based Analysis
- Urban areas generate the highest number of orders and revenue
- Certain regions show high demand but limited restaurant availability

📌 Insight:
There is a supply-demand gap in specific locations, indicating an opportunity for expansion and targeted marketing.

---

## ⚠️ Key Problems Identified

- Over-dependence on a small number of top-performing restaurants
- Low order frequency among a segment of users
- Uneven distribution of restaurants across locations
- High dependence on low-priced items for volume growth

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Restaurant Diversity
- Promote mid-tier restaurants through better visibility on the platform
- Encourage onboarding of new restaurants in high-demand areas

---

### 2. Enhance Customer Retention & Engagement
- Introduce loyalty programs and rewards for frequent users
- Provide personalized recommendations based on user behavior

---

### 3. Optimize Pricing Strategy
- Reduce dependency on blanket discounts
- Use targeted promotions for specific customer segments
- Promote combo meals to increase average order value

---

### 4. Location-Based Expansion
- Expand restaurant network in high-demand, low-supply regions
- Use location-based marketing campaigns to boost underperforming areas

---

## 📈 Expected Business Impact

- Increase in order volume by 10–15%
- Improved customer retention and engagement
- Better balance between revenue and profitability
- Enhanced market coverage across regions

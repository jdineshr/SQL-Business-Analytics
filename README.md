# Swiggy Sales Analysis (SQL Business Analytics)

## 📌 Overview
This project applies **SQL-based business analytics** to explore and analyze Swiggy’s sales data. The goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable insights that help understand customer behavior, restaurant performance, and overall sales trends.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Evaluate **sales performance** across time periods (daily, monthly, quarterly, yearly).
- Identify **top-performing restaurants, cuisines, and delivery zones**.
- Analyze **customer ordering patterns** (frequency, repeat purchases, average order value).
- Assess the impact of **discounts and promotions** on sales.
- Provide **KPIs and dashboards** for decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **SQL (SQL Server)** – core analytics engine  
- **Database schema** – structured tables for orders, customers, restaurants, and delivery partners  
- **Optional Visualization** – Power BI for dashboards  

## 📊 Features
- **Sales Trend Analysis**: Revenue breakdown by time period.  
- **Customer Segmentation**: Loyal vs. one-time customers.  
- **Restaurant Insights**: Ranking by revenue, order volume, and ratings.  
- **Geographical Analysis**: Sales by delivery zones.  
- **Offer Effectiveness**: Discounts and promotions impact.  

## 🚀 Outcomes
- Clear understanding of **business drivers** behind Swiggy’s growth.  
- Data-backed recommendations for **marketing strategies** and **restaurant partnerships**.  
- Scalable SQL queries adaptable to other **food delivery platforms**.
